Tailscale Finder - Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 7, 2026

Overview

Tailscale Finder is a macOS utility that discovers services running on your Tailscale network. Your privacy is important to us.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Tailscale Finder:

  • Does not send any data to BardSec or any third-party servers
  • Does not include analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting
  • Does not track usage patterns or behavior
  • Does not use cookies or any form of persistent tracking

Network Access

Tailscale Finder communicates only with:

  • Tailscale local API (127.0.0.1:41112) — to retrieve the list of devices on your Tailscale network. This is a local connection to the Tailscale app running on your Mac.
  • Your Tailscale devices — to probe open ports and fetch web service titles. All connections are made directly to devices on your private Tailscale network, never to external servers.

No data from these connections is stored persistently or transmitted outside your local machine.

Third-Party Services

Tailscale Finder requires the Tailscale app to be installed and running. We are not affiliated with Tailscale Inc. Please refer to Tailscale's privacy policy for information about their data practices.

Children's Privacy

The application does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version on this page with an updated date.
